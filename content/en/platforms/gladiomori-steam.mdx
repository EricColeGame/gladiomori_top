## Organ-Based Combat and Dismemberment Mechanics

The core philosophy governing Gladio Mori is biological consequence. There are no health bars hovering above gladiators. The game calculates trauma strictly based on impact angle, blade sharpness, kinetic force, and surface coverage. 

A blunt kanabo strike directly against a pauldron may stagger an opponent and crack underlying bone, while a clean draw-cut from a gladius against an exposed throat will induce rapid arterial hemorrhage.

### Anatomical Damage Tiers

| Injury Type | Target Anatomical Area | Mechanical In-Game Consequence | Combat Viability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Muscle Rupture** | Biceps, Calves, Thighs | Diminished attack velocity; reduced footwork speed | Moderate impairment; defense compromised |
| **Arterial Laceration** | Neck, Inner Thigh, Armpit | Steady, uncontrollable bleeding out on the arena floor | Critical timer; must finish duel rapidly |
| **Bone Fracture** | Ribs, Forearms, Knees | Stagger frames increase; inability to hold two-handed grip | Severe; high vulnerability to counter-attacks |
| **Vital Penetration** | Brain, Heart, Lungs | Instant collapse / lethal termination | Duel over immediately |
| **Amputation** | Hands, Forearms, Lower Legs | Dropped weaponry; complete loss of standard locomotion | Desperation state; limited to ground maneuvers |

Because edge alignment matters, wildly flailing your weapon will bounce uselessly off thick hide or plate. Community reports indicate that mastering the angle of contact during weapon collision is far more vital than simply swinging with maximum momentum.

## Tactical Tips for New Arena Combatants

1. **Guard Your Primary Weapon Hand:** Losing your dominant hand means your weapon drops to the ground immediately. When chaining custom combos, avoid extended poses that leave your sword arm outstretched toward your rival.
2. **Armor Gaps Over Armor Plates:** Do not waste energy hacking directly at bronze or iron breastplates. Angle thrusts downward toward femoral arteries or upward underneath the chin rim of the helmet.
3. **Use the Demo First:** Unsure if your system handles the physics workload? Plebeian Studio provides a free playable demo directly on the Steam storefront to test move creation and performance before purchasing.
4. **Replay Your Losses:** Whenever you fall in seconds, use the timeline replay tool. Rotate the free camera to inspect the wound path; you will often discover you leaned into an incoming thrust while initiating an attack.

By approaching each encounter with patience and anatomical awareness, the **Gladio Mori Steam** version transforms from a chaotic physics sandbox into one of the most rewarding skill-based sword fighters available on PC.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the main features of Gladio Mori on Steam?
The Steam version features realistic, organ-based combat without health bars, allowing for tactical dismemberment, muscle fatigue, and bleeding. It also includes an integrated keyframe move editor, full mod.io community support, custom skin import tools, 34 base weapons, and up to four-player cross-play multiplayer.

### Does Gladio Mori on Steam support full cross-play?
Yes. Players fighting on PC via Steam can seamlessly matchmake, host custom lobbies, and duel alongside players running the game on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles.

### Can I run Gladio Mori on a Steam Deck?
Yes, Gladio Mori is currently rated "Playable" on the Steam Deck. It offers native controller support for both Xbox and PlayStation button configurations, holding a steady frame rate with medium visual settings and ragdoll physics enabled.

### How do custom movesets work in Gladio Mori Steam?
Players enter an in-engine animation studio to configure stances, balance, and attack arcs. Once created, these moves can be saved into dynamic combos, tested against target dummies, used in online matches, or shared with other players via the community gallery.
