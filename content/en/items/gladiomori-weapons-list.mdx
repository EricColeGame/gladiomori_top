## Detailed Breakdown of Weapon Categories

To master your build, you must look beyond raw damage numbers and examine how individual weapon categories operate in real sparring scenarios.

### 1. Thrust-Centric Blades: Rapiers and Estocs

Rapiers feature narrow blade profiles that excel at rapid linear lunges. Because physics damage depends heavily on tip velocity, catching an advancing opponent with a counter-thrust frequently results in an instant knockout or fatal torso puncture.

*   **Strengths:** Unmatched forward reach among one-handed weapons; lightning-fast recovery; exceptional point control.
*   **Weaknesses:** Ineffective when opponents close past the crossguard; minimal stopping power against heavy sweeping blades.
*   **Tactical Advice:** Maintain distance. Feint a high thrust to bait a vertical parry, then step offline and drive the point under the armpit.

### 2. Versatile Cut-and-Thrust: Arming Swords & Backswords

The arming sword represents the foundational balance of historical fencing. With balanced mass distribution, it reliably executes horizontal cuts, rising false-edge strikes, and sudden thrusts. The single-edged backsword trade-off introduces thicker blade spines that deliver devastating chopping force at the expense of dual-edge versatility.

```
Arming Sword Tactics:
[Linear Thrust] <---> [Short Edge Parry] <---> [Descending Zornhau Cut]
```

### 3. Dual-Wield Setups: Double Arming Swords & Double Slayers

Dual-wield builds—such as Jisse's Double Arming Sword and the Double Slayer setups—cost substantial loadout points (often 18 or more). However, they grant simultaneous offensive and defensive angles. While one blade traps an opponent's guard, the secondary weapon can slice unguarded vitals. 

Community reports note that dual wielding requires precise camera management and stamina awareness, as mistimed flurries leave both arms fully extended and vulnerable.

### 4. Armor-Breakers: War Hammers & Heavy Maces

As introduced through both official expansions and modded historical collections, the War Hammer introduces a top spike, a rear piercing beak, and a weighted hammer face. Rather than slicing flesh, it bypasses defensive blocks through raw concussive impact, staggering opponents and disorienting their guard recovery.

## Practical Combat Tips: How to Maximize Any Weapon

Regardless of which entry on the **gladiomori weapons list** you bring into the arena, adhering to universal swordplay principles ensures consistent victories.

```
Combat Decision Tree:
[Opponent Swings Wide] 
   ├── Step Backward (Whiff Punish with Rapier Thrust)
   └── Step Forward Under Guard (Close Distance with War Hammer)
```

1. **Control the Distance (Measure):** Never stand at the edge of your opponent's maximum striking range. Either stay completely out of reach or commit to stepping deep into their guard where their blade lacks cutting velocity.
2. **Utilize Held Recovery Frames:** When an attack finishes, keep the input depressed if your weapon ends in a high or outside guard. This turns your weapon into a physical barrier that deflects simultaneous counter-attacks.
3. **Target Unarmored Hitboxes:** Pay close attention to enemy loadouts. If an opponent brings an expensive weapon from the **gladiomori weapons list**, they likely cut points on leg or neck armor. Target those specific body segments.
4. **Feint to Manipulate Angles:** Start an overhead descending strike, abruptly cancel or twist your camera, and convert the momentum into a rising sweep. Physics-based engines reward deceptive rotational energy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best weapon for beginners in the Gladio Mori weapons list?
The **Sword and Shield** combination or the **Single Arming Sword** is widely considered the best choice for novices. Both loadouts offer intuitive defensive coverage, moderate point costs, and forgiving hitboxes while you learn directional footwork and momentum mechanics.

### How do loadout points work in Gladio Mori?
Every piece of equipment—including weapons, off-hand shields, helmets, and body armor—has a specific point value. Players are granted a fixed total budget during character creation. High-end equipment like paired dual blades requires up to 18 points, requiring you to sacrifice protective armor to stay within legal budget limits.

### Can you disarm opponents using specific weapons?
Yes. Weapons with prominent hooks, quillons, or heavy bludgeoning heads (such as the War Hammer or Longsword crossguards) can physically bind and twist an adversary's blade. Delivering a sharp impact directly to an opponent's hands or forearms can force them to drop their weapon entirely.

### Will more weapons be added to the official Gladio Mori weapons list?
Yes. Development updates frequently introduce new historical armaments, ranging from polearms and specialized daggers to alternative fencing swords. Modding support also enables the community to design and test custom historical arms in dedicated servers.
