## How Armor and Collision Physics Work in Combat

Unlike traditional action games that rely on standard hit points and invisible defense stats, *Gladio Mori* operates on pure ragdoll physics, skeletal rigging, and velocity-based blade calculations. A strike does not deal a flat amount of damage; instead, the severity depends on the weapon’s point of impact, blade angle, momentum, and the protective layer covering that specific body part.

When **Gladio Mori armor** pieces are introduced into this physical equation, they fundamentally change how kinetic energy transfers into your fighter.

### Damage Mitigation Types

When a weapon impacts protective gear, the physics engine evaluates three distinct results:

1. **Deflection (Glance):** The blade strikes at an oblique angle against rigid metal or hardened leather, sliding off harmlessly with minimal stamina drain.
2. **Blunt Force Transfer:** Even if plate prevents a blade from cutting flesh, high-momentum weapons (like maces or heavy polearms) transfer kinetic energy directly through the padding, causing staggering or internal bone fractures.
3. **Penetration:** High-velocity thrusting tips find gaps between plates or pierce softer chainmail, inflicting bleeding and structural damage.

### Mobility vs. Protection Trade-Offs

Choosing heavy protective equipment changes your tactical pacing. In *Gladio Mori*, your fighter's center of mass directly affects your animation keyframes and turn rates. 

* **Weight Distribution:** Heavy chest pieces raise your center of mass, making your gladiator more susceptible to sweeping leg kicks and body throws.
* **Stamina and Recovery:** While the game focuses on realistic muscle exertion, heavier limbs slow down your custom keyframe resets, leaving you open after a missed swing.
* **Visual Obstruction:** Closed-face helmets like armets or barbutes limit situational awareness in multiplayer free-for-all matches.

### Reading Opponent Stances

Player experience shows that aggressive opponents often telegraph their lethal strikes through subtle stance adjustments:

* **High Overhead Guard:** Suggests a crushing downward cut aimed at your unarmored skull. Counter by stepping forward inside the arc or raising a horizontal cross-block.
* **Low Thrusting Posture:** Signals a targeted strike to your midsection or groin. Step laterally off the centerline to let the thrust pass harmlessly into empty air.
* **Wide Sweeping Stance:** Designed to slice legs and ruin footing. Hop or quickly backstep to punish the long recovery window.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Gladio Mori Armor

### How can I unlock Gladio Mori armor in the Steam demo?
Currently, full sets of **Gladio Mori armor** cannot be unlocked through standard gameplay in the free public demo. The clips circulating online featuring knights, katanas, and plate gear are taken from closed developer branches and internal testing sessions. ### Does wearing armor eliminate one-hit kills in Gladio Mori?
No. Because combat calculations rely on dynamic physical momentum and realistic strike angles, armor significantly reduces the likelihood of instant death from glancing cuts, but it does not make you invulnerable. A high-speed thrust directly into an eye slit, throat gap, or unarmored armpit joint can still end a match instantly.

### Will Gladio Mori feature modular armor customization?
Developer previews indicate that equipment customization will be modular. Players are expected to mix and match individual pieces—such as helmets, spaulders, breastplates, and greaves—allowing fighters to balance protection, swing velocity, and aesthetic style according to their personal combat preferences.
