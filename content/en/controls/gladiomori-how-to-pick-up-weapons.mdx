## Core Interaction Mechanics: Gladio Mori How to Pick Up Weapons

Unlike conventional hack-and-slash games where characters magnetically snap nearby swords into their hands, *Gladio Mori* uses realistic, physics-driven reach and interaction systems. ### Step-by-Step Retrieval Method

Executing a clean ground pickup during a duel requires strict positioning:

1. **Neutralize Threat Spacing:** Never look down at the floor while an opponent is winding up a swing. Dash or create at least two weapon-lengths of distance.
2. **Lower Your Center of Mass:** Crouch to reduce the physical distance between your gladiator’s palms and the weapon hilt.
3. **Align the Reticle / Torso:** Turn your torso so your main arm extends directly above the weapon hilt rather than the blade tip.
4. **Engage the Pickup Command:** Hold the interact key until the ragdoll physics latch your fingers around the shaft.
5. **Reset Guard Stance:** Release the crouch input and immediately raise your weapon to a defensive guard.

### What is the exact input for gladiomori how to pick up weapons on PC?
By default, you must approach the fallen weapon, crouch using `Ctrl` or `C` to reach ground level, look directly at the grip, and hold the interaction key (`E` or `F`). Ensure your hand is unencumbered so your character can latch onto the weapon shaft.

### Can you throw a weapon and pick it back up in Gladio Mori?
Yes. Tactical throwing is an effective method to interrupt heavy wind-ups or break an enemy's guard. Once the thrown weapon embeds in the ground or bounces off armor, you can sprint forward, slide into range, and execute the standard pickup maneuver to re-arm yourself.

### Does taking arm damage prevent you from picking up two-handed weapons?
Yes. Severe muscular cuts or fractures to either arm will prevent your gladiator from effectively wielding heavy two-handed weaponry. In those moments, look for light daggers, shortswords, or one-handed axes that your functional limb can still carry.
