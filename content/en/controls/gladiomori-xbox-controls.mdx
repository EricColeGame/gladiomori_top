## Gladio Mori Xbox Controller Layout Overview

Gladio Mori completely abandons arcade-style combos in favor of authentic, momentum-based physics. Because of this, the default Xbox controller profile maps movement, weapon trajectory, and defensive positioning directly to dual analog sticks and shoulder bumpers. 

Understanding how your controller triggers interact with the game's dynamic animation engine will keep you from overextending your blade.

| Xbox Controller Input | Primary In-Game Function | Combat Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Left Stick (LS)** | Gladiator Movement / Footwork | Closing gaps, pivoting hips, managing engagement distance |
| **Right Stick (RS)** | Blade Angle / Torso Orientation | Directing swings, aiming thrusts at weak spots |
| **Right Trigger (RT)** | Primary Strike / Release Attack | Heavy windup and forward slashing |
| **Right Bumper (RB)** | Quick Thrust / Light Attack | Piercing unarmored joints and throats |
| **Left Trigger (LT)** | Defensive Guard / Active Parrying | Angling shield or blade against incoming hits |
| **Left Bumper (LB)** | Modifier / Stance Shift | Adjusting posture or initiating alternate attacks |
| **A Button** | Dash / Evasive Footwork | Quick repositioning away from lethal trajectories |
| **B Button** | Feint / Cancel Animation | Resetting weapon momentum before committed swings |
| **X Button** | Grapple / Push | Off-balancing shielded foes at close quarters |
| **Y Button** | Target Lock / Stance Reset | Realigning camera center on active adversary |
| **D-Pad** | Tactical Callouts / Equipment Toggle | Quick equipment switches and local HUD adjustments |
| **View Button** | Scoreboard / Match Status | Reviewing organ status and arena stats |
| **Menu Button** | Pause / Settings Menu | Remapping inputs and tuning deadzones |

Mastering the interaction between the Left Stick (momentum) and the Right Stick (weapon swing angle) represents the core learning curve. Community reports consistently note that treating the controller like a flight simulator—where your whole body guides the weapon—yields far better results than rapid button mashing.

## Defensive Systems: Parrying, Blocking, and Footwork

Offense only wins matches if you survive the initial clash. Because armor only covers designated body parts, defensive inputs must be proactive rather than passive. Holding your guard with Left Trigger will not save you if the enemy's blade slips underneath your pauldron.

### Blocking vs. Active Parrying on Xbox Controllers

| Defensive Maneuver | Controller Command | Risk Level | Tactical Payoff |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Static Block** | Hold **LT** | Low | Absorbs blow on shield/armor; costs weapon stability |
| **Directional Deflection** | Hold **LT** + Angle **RS** | Medium | Deflects blade away from your centerline |
| **Timed Riposte** | Tap **LT** right before impact | High | Staggers opponent, opening an unobstructed vital target |
| **Evasive Pivot** | **LS** Back/Side + Tap **A** | Medium | Completely evades contact, preserves weapon stamina |

When executing a timed riposte, player experience indicates that watching your opponent's hips rather than the tip of their blade offers a much clearer indicator of release timing.

## Optimizing Deadzones, Sensitivity, and Console Settings

Because Gladio Mori runs at 60 fps+ in 4K on Xbox Series X|S, input lag is minimal. However, default controller settings might feel loose if you have not adjusted stick curves to account for physical blade momentum.

| Setting | Recommended Value | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RS Horizontal Sensitivity** | 6.5 – 7.5 | Allows rapid blade repositioning without sacrificing precision |
| **RS Vertical Sensitivity** | 5.0 – 6.0 | Prevents accidental downward slashes during level thrusts |
| **Inner Deadzone (LS & RS)** | 3% – 5% | Eliminates stick drift while maintaining immediate reaction times |
| **Outer Deadzone** | 95% | Ensures full swing momentum is reached with minimal travel |
| **Trigger Activation Threshold**| Instant (Hair Trigger) | Minimizes strike initiation delay on RT/RB strikes |

Community reports highlight that lowering your Right Stick inner deadzone drastically improves micro-aiming when trying to target narrow gaps in heavy plate armor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I remap the Gladio Mori Xbox controls completely?
Yes. Gladio Mori offers full in-game controller remapping under the Options menu. Additionally, you can utilize the native Xbox Accessories application on your console to create custom profiles, adjust stick response curves, and remap physical buttons.

### How do I trigger community movesets using an Xbox controller?
Once you download or build a custom moveset in the editor, bind it to an active loadout slot. These animations replace your default swings and can be triggered using your standard strike inputs (**RT**, **RB**, or modifier combinations like **LB + RT**).

### Does Gladio Mori support mouse and keyboard on Xbox consoles?
While Gladio Mori supports keyboard and mouse natively on PC, the console version on Xbox Series X|S is fully optimized for standard Xbox wireless controllers. Check the game's latest patch notes in the main menu to see if keyboard support has been expanded for console users.

### Why do my strikes feel sluggish using the default gladiomori xbox controls?
In Gladio Mori, strikes rely on authentic physics and muscle health rather than arcade frames. If your character's arm muscles have suffered trauma, your swing speeds are physically reduced. Additionally, swinging against your body's natural momentum will slow your blade down—use the Left Stick to pivot your hips into each blow for maximum velocity.
