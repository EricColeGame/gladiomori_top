## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes the gladiomori daily duel different from regular arena matches?
The daily duel environment forces players to face varying community loadouts, tailored constraints, and competitive participants who optimize their movesets specifically for quick, lethal engagements. ### How do I stop getting disarmed during duels?
Disarming occurs when an opponent shatters your forearm bones, severs forearm muscles, or makes direct blade contact with your hands. Equip steel vambraces, avoid leaving your weapon extended after missed strikes, and edit your custom moves to include rapid weapon pullbacks.

### Can I practice against AI before jumping into online fights?
Yes. The solo campaign and local practice lobbies let you test custom movesets and weapon combinations against computer-controlled bots. It is highly recommended to test the strike angles and recovery times of any new community moveset against AI before risking your record in live competitive bouts.

### What is the fastest way to end a daily duel?
The most reliable method for an instant victory is landing a direct thrust into an unarmored eye socket, open visor, or the unprotected throat. Piercing the brain or heart causes an immediate fatal knockout, bypassing limb damage and bleeding stages entirely.
