## Combat Mechanics: How Weapons Deal Damage

Combat in Gladio Mori rejects traditional RPG damage numbers in favor of raw physics and anatomical trauma. To win duels consistently, fighters must understand the three core wounding mechanics governed by your weapon choices:

*   **Cutting & Dismemberment:** Edge alignment determines whether a blade merely glances off flesh or cleanly slices through limbs. Following recent combat updates, multi-target cutting allows vertical strikes to slice cleanly through connecting body parts, tracking the realistic swing path of the blade.
*   **Piercing & Organ Targeting:** Thrusting attacks bypass broad surface armor to target vital organs like the heart, lungs, and primary arteries. Puncturing an artery triggers rapid bleeding, drastically shortening the duel.
*   **Blunt Force & Concussion:** Introduced to expand heavy combat playstyles, blunt damage causes concussive trauma, fractures bones, and knocks opponents off balance without requiring an edge. Heavy axes can also deliver crushing blunt impacts depending on point-of-contact mechanics.

```
[Swing Trajectory] ──> [Edge Alignment / Mass Velocity] ──> [Hitbox Contact]

         ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
         ▼                    ▼                    ▼
   Cutting Edge         Piercing Tip         Blunt Surface
   (Dismemberment)      (Arterial Rupture)   (Fractures/Stuns)
```

Understanding how these mechanics interface with various **Gladio Mori weapons** allows players to craft custom animation profiles that maximize kinetic momentum.

## Best Moveset Configurations for Gladio Mori Weapons

Because the game allows you to customize your animation sequences frame by frame, your weapon's physical dimensions must guide your animation design. Equipping powerful **gladiomori weapons** means nothing if your custom animations don't generate sufficient tip velocity.

### Optimizing the Podao for Dismemberment
1. **Initiation Frame:** Begin with a low-to-high diagonal sweep to sweep underneath incoming horizontal thrusts.
2. **Torque Step:** Rotate the character's hips 45 degrees into the cut to maximize rotational momentum.
3. **Follow-Through:** Ensure the blade's arc completes across the opponent's torso line to trigger the dual-hitbox cleave mechanic.

### Spear and Gou-Rang Defensive Counter Poking
1. **Guard Hold:** Keep the Gou-Rang angled outward toward the opponent's main hand.
2. **Catch & Bind:** Animate a subtle outward hooking flick to deflect incoming swings.
3. **Piston Thrust:** Fire a direct linear spear stab aimed squarely at the throat or subclavian artery.

## Practical Combat Tips for Duels

*   **Watch the Point of Impact:** Remember that heavy cutting weapons like the Dane Axe transition into blunt damage if you strike with the shaft rather than the blade head. Gauge your distances carefully.
*   **Target Exposed Limbs:** If your opponent utilizes long wind-up animations, design a rapid jab moveset using faster **gladiomori weapons** to strike their weapon arm mid-swing, causing them to drop their guard.
*   **Respect Dismemberment Trajectories:** Cuts now follow blade angles much more strictly. When facing slashing weapons, crouch-weaving under high horizontal arcs is significantly more effective than back-stepping.
*   **Utilize Shield Pinning:** With weapon-and-shield combinations, press forward aggressively against long polearms to trap them in close-quarters combat where their reach becomes a liability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which of the Gladio Mori weapons is best for beginners?
The **Podao** is widely considered the most accessible weapon for newcomers. Its broad sweeping arcs, forgiving cutting hitboxes, and extended reach allow new fighters to learn spacing and dismemberment mechanics without needing frame-perfect thrust accuracy.

### How does blunt damage work compared to sharp damage?
Sharp damage focuses on severing limbs and puncturing vital organs to induce fatal blood loss. Blunt damage from weapons like the Kanabo and Mace bypasses traditional deflection angles to deal internal skeletal trauma, breaking bones, staggering animations, and cracking skulls without requiring clean edge alignment.

### Can all Gladio Mori weapons sever limbs?
No. Blunt weapons like the Mace and Kanabo cannot dismember opponents, as they are engineered exclusively for impact and crushing trauma. Edged weapons such as the Podao and Dane Axe excel at severing arms, legs, and heads, while thrusting spears focus primarily on deep puncture wounds.

### Does weapon weight affect custom moveset animation speeds?
Yes. Weapon mass directly influences the kinetic physics of your custom animations. Attempting to swing a heavy Kanabo or Dane Axe with an ultra-fast animation profile will result in erratic physics, reduced strike velocity, and sluggish recovery times compared to lighter weapons.
