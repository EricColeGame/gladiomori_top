### How Physics Dictate Weapon Damage

Weapons do not simply roll a damage number upon contact. Instead, damage calculates based on:

1. **Velocity at Impact**: Swinging a heavy blade with wide hip and torso rotation creates higher kinetic force than a wrist flick.
2. **Contact Point**: Hitting with the sweet spot (upper third of a blade) produces maximum severing damage, whereas hitting near the crossguard delivers far less force.
3. **Target Anatomy**: Striking soft tissue yields instant incapacitation, while striking bone can redirect your weapon's momentum.

### One-Handed Weapons Breakdown

One-handed blades, led by the signature Roman Gladius, excel at close-quarters infighting. Because these weapons require minimal leverage, your character can initiate rapid thrusts from tight defensive stances.

- **Gladius**: The quintessential close-range weapon. It favors tight thrusting animations aimed at the abdomen, throat, and thighs. Its short reach is offset by almost zero recovery delay, allowing you to sidestep sweeping strikes and punish overextended opponents.
- **Hunting Dagger**: Designed primarily as a parrying tool or secondary off-hand weapon, daggers allow you to deflect incoming blows while stepping inside maximum reach to deliver critical punctures.

### Two-Handed Weapons Breakdown

Two-handed weapons provide the stopping power necessary to sever limbs and force stagger states.

- **Longsword**: The most versatile tool in the game. It balances defensive guard transitions with crushing overhead cuts and long-range thrusts. If you are learning the animation timeline editor, the Longsword is the best baseline weapon for tuning swing speeds.
- **Zweihänder**: The ultimate zoning tool. Its immense length keeps single or multiple foes at bay. However, player experience indicates that missing a strike with a Zweihänder leaves a long recovery window where you are vulnerable to fast thrusts.

## Optimizing Custom Movesets for Different Weapons

The core feature of this gladiatorial sim is the custom animation editor. Building the right attack sequence depends heavily on the weapon you bring into the arena. Equipping a heavy blade requires different animation timing than wielding a light shortsword.

```
[Start Stance] -> [Anticipation / Windup] -> [Release / Kinetic Apex] -> [Follow-Through] -> [Recovery Guard]
```

Use the following configuration guidelines to optimize your animations according to weapon weight:

| Parameter | Light Weapons (Gladius/Dagger) | Medium Weapons (Longsword) | Heavy Weapons (Zweihänder/Halberd) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Windup Frames** | 4 – 8 frames | 10 – 16 frames | 18 – 28 frames |
| **Kinetic Apex Speed** | High initial acceleration | Progressive acceleration | Heavy momentum build-up |
| **Torso Rotation** | Minimal (15° – 30°) | Moderate (45° – 60°) | Maximum (90° – 130°) |
| **Recovery Window** | Near instantaneous | Short to moderate | Long (requires defensive retreat) |
| **Footwork Shift** | Quick pivot / dash | Standard lunge / pass step | Deep passing step / wide stance |

### Key Guidelines for Animation Tuning

- **Align Hip Rotation with Weapon Mass**: Do not swing a two-handed weapon using only arm bones. Turn the hips and spine first to create realistic torque and maximum blade velocity.
- **Avoid Overextending the Center of Gravity**: If your strike pulls your character's chest too far beyond their lead knee, an enemy deflection will knock you to the sand.
- **Incorporate Reset Steps**: Always end an attack animation with your feet beneath your shoulders and your weapon returning to a protective high or middle guard.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I unlock more weapons in the demo version?
Based on player experience and developer announcements, the free demo features a restricted armory (primarily the gladius and longsword). Exploring the complete **gladiomori all weapons** library requires the full commercial version of the game upon release.

### Does weapon weight affect movement and sprint speed?
Yes. Weapon weight alters your character's center of mass, turn rate, and dash acceleration. Heavier weapons like the Zweihänder require deliberate footwork and wider turning radiuses, whereas one-handed blades permit rapid repositioning and faster direction changes.

### Can weapons break or dull during extended duels?
Weapons do not currently feature durability degradation, but violent blade-on-blade collisions can alter attack trajectories, cause weapon bounces, or tear weapons from a combatant's hands if their grip strength is compromised.

### What is the most effective weapon for beginners?
The Longsword is universally recommended for new players. It offers an ideal compromise between reach, swing speed, and defensive coverage, making it easy to learn the physics-based animation system before specializing in extreme weapons.
