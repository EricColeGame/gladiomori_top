## Top 5 Tactical Tips for Winning Duels

Regardless of your chosen steel, general combat awareness determines who leaves the arena alive. Follow these core rules to get the most out of the **gladiomori best weapon** in your lineup:

1. **Aim for Unprotected Joints**: Armor and muscle density reduce raw damage. Direct your weapon points toward the neck, inner elbows, and groin for high-lethality hits.
2. **Control the Centerline**: Keep your weapon's point aimed directly between your opponent’s shoulders. Forcing them to move around your tip limits their offensive angles.
3. **Never Over-Rotate**: In a physics engine, spinning attacks leave your back and base of the skull exposed. Tight, controlled strikes consistently outperform wild swings.
4. **Feint to Draw Parries**: Start an animation, cancel or steer the blade off-course, and slip past their active block to land a clean blow.
5. **Manage Your Balance**: Heavy swings pull your torso forward. If your feet are not positioned securely, an opponent’s parry can send you sprawling to the floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the gladiomori best weapon for beginners?
The Longsword is widely considered the best weapon for newcomers. It features a generous balance between reach, speed, and blocking surface. This broad margin for error lets new players learn distance control and timing without being severely punished for minor mistakes.

### Can light weapons parry heavy polearms effectively?
Yes, but physics and leverage matter. A small blade like a dagger or light rapier must parry close to its hilt (the forte) against the haft of a polearm. Trying to parry a heavy downward poleaxe blow with the tip of a rapier will often collapse your guard and result in massive damage.

### Does weapon weight affect footwork and dodge speed?
Yes. Heavier equipment shifts your character's center of mass and increases movement inertia. Running, stopping, and pivoting take longer when wielding massive polearms compared to moving with a lightweight side sword or single-handed rapier.

### Why do thrusting weapons score so many instant kills?
In combat simulations, piercing damage focuses all kinetic force onto a microscopic point, allowing blades to penetrate vitals like the brain stem, heart, or carotid artery with minimal resistance. This makes pinpoint thrusting weapons exceptionally deadly in skilled hands.
