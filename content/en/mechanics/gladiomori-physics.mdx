## The Core Fundamentals of Gladio Mori Physics

Most fighting games rely on "active frames"—invisible damage boxes tied to predefined keyframe animations. In contrast, the simulation here operates on continuous physical interactions. Your gladiator’s strikes carry actual mass, velocity, and leverage calculated from foot placement, torso rotation, and wrist articulation.

When your weapon makes contact, the game assesses the angle of incidence, relative speed, and point of impact along the blade. Striking near the tip yields higher linear velocity, while hitting closer to the guard generates more rotational leverage to displace an opponent's defense.

```
Linear Swing Momentum = Weapon Mass × Angular Velocity at Point of Impact
```

Because of this active simulation, every physical surface interacts dynamically:

* **Blade-on-Blade Friction:** Crossing swords causes weapons to slide, bind, or deflect based on angle and applied force.
* **Momentum Transfer:** Heavy swings that miss pull your gladiator forward, exposing your unarmored flanks.
* **Anatomical Resistance:** Cutting through soft flesh offers little resistance, whereas hitting bone or steel transfers shockwaves back into your fighter's wrists.

## Footing, Balance, and Environmental Friction

Swords do not win duels in isolation; your lower body dictates everything. The simulation models ground friction and center of mass constantly. Stepping into soft sand, damp soil, or blood-slicked stone directly alters your gladiator’s traction.

When you execute a heavy cleave, your center of mass shifts forward. If an opponent steps inside your range and shoves you, the existing forward momentum amplifies their push, causing your character to stumble or collapse into a ragdoll state.

## Advanced Combat Tactics Powered by In-Game Physics

Exploiting the detailed **Gladio Mori physics** framework allows skilled fighters to defeat heavily armored opponents through mechanical leverage rather than brute force. By manipulating angles, you can neutralize threatening weapons before they generate lethal velocity.

### Essential Physics-Driven Tactics

* **The Bind and Lever:** Trap your opponent’s blade against your guard near the hilt. Because you possess superior mechanical leverage, twisting your wrists can force their weapon point to the ground.
* **Shield Rim Displacement:** Instead of passively absorbing hits, strike the opponent's weapon arm mid-swing with your shield boss to kill their swing velocity before impact.
* **Hollow-Foot Dodging:** Step your lead foot back just as a slash enters its terminal phase. The blade will whiff harmlessly through empty air, dragging the attacker off-balance.
* **Point Sniping:** Against charging enemies, hold your weapon steady in a thrust stance. The opponent’s own forward velocity gets added to your weapon’s impact equation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does weapon weight affect swing speed in Gladio Mori physics?
Weapon weight directly dictates angular acceleration. Heavier weapons like greatswords or polearms require more physical frames to reach maximum velocity, meaning their early startup phase deals minimal impact damage. However, once full momentum is achieved, they generate massive kinetic force capable of staggering blocking opponents.

### Can armor completely negate blade damage?
Yes. If an incoming slash does not generate sufficient kinetic pressure to pierce or deform the armor material, the strike registers as a glancing blow or surface deflection. However, blunt force trauma is still transmitted through the padding, which can drain your stamina or cause minor concussive stagger.

### Why do attacks sometimes deal negligible damage even on direct hits?
Damage is computed using contact velocity and the weapon's specific cutting edge. If your attack connects during the windup phase (before the blade gathers speed) or hits with the flat side rather than the edge, the **Gladio Mori physics** system classifies the collision as an unaligned or low-velocity hit, resulting in negligible trauma.

### Does player movement speed amplify attack power?
Yes. Forward movement velocity is added to the weapon's striking speed at the precise moment of impact. Slicing while sprinting or lunging increases the total kinetic energy delivered to the target, while retreating reduces the relative force of incoming attacks.
